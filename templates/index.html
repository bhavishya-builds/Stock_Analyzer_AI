What is Momentum (RSI)?

N/A

Measures if a stock is 'Oversold' (maybe cheap) or 'Overbought' (maybe expensive).

RSI stands for Relative Strength Index. It's a momentum indicator that moves between 0 and 100.

How to use this: Traders look for two key levels: 70 (Overbought) and 30 (Oversold). A stock at 70 might be too expensive. A stock at 30 might be too cheap.

What is the Price Trend (EMA)?

N/A

Shows if the stock is in a general 'Upward Trend' or 'Downward Trend'.

EMA stands for Exponential Moving Average. It's a line on the chart that smooths out price data to show the trend.

How to use this: When a Fast line crosses ABOVE a Slow line, it's a 'Bullish' signal. You can see this in detail on the 'Pro View' chart.
